## What About the Camera Error?

**The error is your browser blocking camera access.**

This is normal browser security. You can:

**Option A (Easy):** Use "Upload Photo" instead → Works immediately

**Option B (Hard):** Enable camera permission:
1. Click 🔒 in browser address bar
2. Camera → Allow
3. Refresh page

**Option A is recommended.**
