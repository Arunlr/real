# ⚡ 5-MINUTE SOLUTION

## Step 1: Test Camera (2 minutes)

### Go to: `/test-camera`

1. Click the big blue "Test Camera Now" button
2. Browser will show permission prompt
3. Click **"Allow"** in the prompt
4. You should see live video

### ✅ If video appears:
**You're done! Camera works. Skip to Step 4.**

### ❌ If error appears:
**Go to Step 2.**

---

## Step 2: Fix Permission (1 minute)

### Look at the TOP of your browser window (address bar)

You'll see one of these:
- 🔒 Lock icon (click it)
- 🎥 Camera icon (click it)
- ⚠️ Warning icon (click it)

### Click the icon, then:

**Chrome/Edge:**
1. Find "Camera" in dropdown
2. Click and select "Allow"
3. Dropdown closes
4. Go back to `/test-camera`
5. Click "Try Again"

**Firefox:**
1. Click ">" next to "Connection"
2. Find "Use the Camera"
3. Select "Allow"
4. Go back to `/test-camera`
5. Click "Try Again"

**Safari:**
1. Safari menu → Settings → Websites
2. Click "Camera"
3. Find your site
4. Change to "Allow"
5. Go back to `/test-camera`
6. Click "Try Again"
